THE TROPHY THAT WALKED — SEASON 3
Teacher reference; full story spoilers.

The same campus as Season 2, on a later rainy school event day. The old covered passage remains closed. A new small leak above the trophy display is handled by staff.

Before lessons, Ms Diop hides a small speaker inside the base of the real school trophy. Its recorded invitation says, “Find the SUN.” The sun is the courtyard event sign. This is her planned surprise invitation to a school celebration, not a planned theft or emergency.

At 9:00, staff notice a leak above the trophy stand and leave a note asking for the trophy to be moved somewhere dry. Chidi, in his green hoodie, carries the real STAR-badge trophy to a blue box at first aid. He is helping, not stealing. He has not yet told everyone where it is.

At 9:05, Laia tests her remote-controlled duck robot D4. The duck carries her cardboard ROUND-badge cup to the library for a robot show. A camera catches it near the now-empty display. People think it took the school trophy, but the two cups were never the same object. Laia did not create the leak or move the real trophy.

Chapter 1: Students match a book sign to the library and D4 to Laia. Twist: the giant duck is a robot, with nobody inside. Laia says its cup is not the real trophy.

Chapter 2: Students compare the circle and star badges, then match a green sleeve to Chidi. Twist: there were two cups. Chidi moved the real one before the duck arrived.

Chapter 3: Students match the cross sign to first aid and the water picture to the office’s dry-room instruction. Twist: the apparent thief saved the trophy. When the box is opened, the trophy’s speaker plays its invitation. The game presents this as a story event; students do not physically open an in-world box or trigger audio.

Chapter 4: Students match the sun sign to the courtyard and the note’s “PE teacher” to Ms Diop. Twist: the talking trophy contains a speaker, and its message is a party invitation. The robot returns for its real show, throwing paper confetti. Chidi brings the real trophy. The class solved the confusion by sharing pictures, not by accusing people.

No supernatural event, kidnapping, student culprit assignment, secret fifth chapter or unexplained swap. Only three named characters: Laia, Chidi and Ms Diop. Each reveal answers the current question and starts one clear next question.
