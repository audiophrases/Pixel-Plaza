PIXEL PLAZA - SEASON 2
PROTOCOL 8:15 - THE MISSING STUDENT
Storyline draft | 3 chapters

SETTING
A fictionalized version of Institut Escola Coma-ruga during a violent Mediterranean winter storm. The story features both students and teachers, conflicting emergency instructions, a missing student, and a secret involving school safety.

GENRE AND FORMAT
School mystery / suspense thriller, suitable for teenage English learners (A2-B1).
Three chapters of approximately 20-25 minutes each.
Two cliffhangers, followed by a final revelation.

MAIN CHARACTERS
- Karina Gonzalvez: A student who has discovered evidence of a serious safety problem. She disappears during the storm.
- Youssef El Amrani: Karina's best friend. He knows more than he admits and feels guilty for leaving her alone.
- Ms Awa Diop: The PE teacher. She has been seen with Karina but denies it.
- Ms Andreea Ionescu: A teacher who defends Ms Diop and knows part of the truth.
- Mr El Idrissi: The deputy head. He ignored a safety warning and tries to conceal that decision.
- Laia Garcia: A student who saved a screenshot of the conflicting announcements. She helps the investigators reconstruct the timeline.
- Chidi Okafor: A student who noticed the blocked passage near the sports hall and has an important piece of firsthand evidence.
- Ms Irina Sokolova: A maths teacher who kept a copy of an earlier maintenance notice. Her document helps establish when the school first knew about the danger.
- The players: Student investigators with different clues, who must compare evidence to understand what happened.

CHAPTER 1 - THE MISSING STUDENT
A violent storm is hitting the school. At 8:15 a.m., different parts of the campus receive contradictory emergency instructions. Some students are told to remain inside, while others are told to move to another area.

During roll call, Karina is missing. Laia has a screenshot showing that the two emergency announcements were posted at almost the same time.

Her best friend, Youssef, says he hasn't seen her, but his nervous behaviour raises suspicion. Ms Diop insists that students must stay away from the sports hall. Why is she so determined to keep everyone away?

Students investigate the warnings, collect clues and compare accounts. Chidi mentions a blocked passage near the sports hall, but nobody is sure whether it matters.

CLIFFHANGER 1:
Security footage shows Ms Diop entering the sports hall with Karina shortly before she disappeared. But she has denied seeing her.

CHAPTER 2 - EVERYONE HAS A SECRET
Suspicion falls on Ms Diop. Ms Ionescu defends her, but refuses to explain why. Youssef finally admits that Karina had been investigating a serious safety problem that the school had ignored. She asked him to help, but he got scared and backed out.

Further evidence shows that Ms Diop had reported the danger days earlier. Ms Sokolova has a dated copy of that notice. Somebody in school management chose not to act on her warning.

Students must work out who knew about the danger and why the adults' stories don't match. Laia's screenshot and Ms Sokolova's document establish a revealing timeline.

CLIFFHANGER 2:
A message arrives from Karina's account:
"I'm safe. But the person you should be afraid of isn't Ms Diop. It's the person who told her to keep quiet."

CHAPTER 3 - THE TRUTH
The players reconstruct the timeline and uncover what happened.

Mr El Idrissi ignored a maintenance warning about the covered passage near the sports hall. When the storm arrived, Mr El Idrissi tried to conceal that decision.

Ms Diop recognized the danger and helped Karina reach a safe area, but lied about seeing her because she feared the consequences of disobeying Mr El Idrissi. Karina's phone stopped working, leaving her unable to contact her classmates. Ms Ionescu knew that Ms Diop had tried to protect Karina but was reluctant to challenge school management.

Youssef must admit that he abandoned his friend when she needed him. Ms Diop and Ms Ionescu must decide whether to tell the whole truth. Mr El Idrissi's attempt to hide the ignored warning is exposed.

Chidi's observations, Laia's screenshot and Ms Sokolova's notice complete the timeline. The students combine their evidence, identify the unsafe area and communicate their findings so the school staff can resolve the emergency safely.

ENDING
Karina is safe. The central mystery is solved, but the consequences remain: damaged trust, guilt, and difficult choices about speaking up. The story ends with a question: When telling the truth could cost you something, will you still do it?

WHY THIS VERSION WORKS
- Human drama drives the mystery: friendship, fear, betrayal, loyalty and responsibility.
- Students and teachers both have secrets and something to lose.
- Chapter 1 ends by casting suspicion on a teacher.
- Chapter 2 changes the players' understanding of the suspected teacher and points towards a cover-up.
- Chapter 3 reveals the full truth and brings the danger and relationships to a resolution.
- Mr El Idrissi can be written as a deliberately manipulative character, not merely someone who made a mistake, while keeping the story age-appropriate.
