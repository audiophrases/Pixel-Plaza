# Evidence artwork

Document cards are editable SVG artwork from `../../build-evidence.mjs`, rendered to compact PNG files. Their essential wording is also in the clue text and Catalan help. Rebuild artwork before rebuilding the pack.

The CCTV illustration was generated with the built-in image tool. `cctv-original.png` preserves the unmodified generation. `cctv.jpg` is a smaller JPEG copy for embedding in the mission, without narrative changes.

## Exact generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create one landscape evidence illustration for a fictional teenage school mystery, Protocol 8:15. A grainy desaturated blue-gray school CCTV view from a high corner, looking at the entrance to an indoor sports hall on a rainy morning. An adult Black woman teacher in a practical rain jacket walks calmly beside a teenage girl in ordinary school clothes and backpack, both entering the hall, viewed from behind at a distance. Neither person faces the camera. Nothing menacing or violent. Mediterranean secondary school interior, damp doorway, muted amber light from hall, subtle security-camera grain, understated mature graphic-novel rendering. The frame shows ONLY the main entrance, with no back exit visible, no damaged passage, no first-aid room, no future evidence. Minimal small camera overlay text exactly 'CAM 03' and '08:07'. No other text, no labels identifying people, no logos, no decorative border. Landscape 3:2 composition; evidence must imply entry only, not motives or guilt.
