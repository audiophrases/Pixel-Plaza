# Protocol 8:15 — teacher guide (spoilers)

Karina is missing after a storm at school. In three short chapters the class finds out where she went, why a teacher lied about it, and who told her to keep quiet. Everything is invented for the classroom story.

## How it plays: one picture each

Every student has one private picture, which matches their role:

| Role | Picture shows |
| --- | --- |
| Message checker | a message, an email or a receipt |
| People checker | a person speaking (a speech bubble) |
| Document checker | a plan, a warning or a staff list |
| Place checker | a photo, a map or a timeline |

**Every question needs two pictures held by two different roles.** The question says whose: “Ask the Message checker and the Document checker.” So the team must tell each other what their pictures show, in the 🤝 Team chat or out loud in English, before anyone can answer.

The same three steps, every chapter:

1. Look at your picture.
2. Tell your team what it shows. Ask: “What is your picture?”
3. Answer together in the Journal.

Each chapter has two questions, then one short piece of writing done together. Questions open one at a time. In teams, one answer counts for the whole team. A team of three is missing one role: that role’s picture reaches the team as a *town notice*, so nothing gets stuck.

Places on the map (courtyard, library, sports hall, office, first aid) have small extra clues when students walk by. They are story colour; no answer needs them.

## Say this before play (1 minute)

“Karina is missing. Each of you has one picture. Tell your team what your picture shows, then answer together.”

Then show one picture on the projector and model it: “My picture shows two messages. One says stay in classrooms. One says go to the sports hall. What is your picture?”

## Chapter 1 — Karina Is Missing

Opens with the storm picture.

| Question | Pictures needed | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Which message is wrong? | Message checker: the two 8:15 messages · Document checker: the storm plan (“stay in your classroom”) | **Go to the sports hall** |
| Where did Karina go? | People checker: Youssef, “Karina went to take a photo of the broken roof” · Place checker: photo of the broken roof over the covered passage | **The covered passage** |

Write together: “The wrong message says… Karina went to…”

Ending: a security camera saw Karina at 8:07 with a teacher.

## Chapter 2 — Why Did Ms Diop Lie?

Opens with the camera picture: Ms Diop and Karina at 8:07. Ms Diop says she did not see her.

| Question | Pictures needed | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who warned the school about the roof first? | Document checker: Ms Diop’s warning, Friday 9:20 · Message checker: the office receipt, Friday 9:45, “Repair delayed” | **Ms Diop** |
| Where did Ms Diop take Karina? | People checker: Ms Ionescu, “Ms Diop took Karina out by the back door at 8:12” · Place checker: map, back door → safe path → first-aid room | **The first-aid room** |

Write together: “Ms Diop lied, but she… She took Karina to…”

Ending: Karina’s message at 8:28, “I’m safe. Someone told Ms Diop to keep quiet.”

## Chapter 3 — Who Said “Keep Quiet”?

Opens with Karina’s message.

| Question | Pictures needed | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who told Ms Diop to keep quiet? | People checker: Ms Diop, “The deputy head told me to keep quiet” · Document checker: staff list, deputy head = Mr El Idrissi | **Mr El Idrissi** |
| When did the school first know about the broken roof? | Message checker: Mr El Idrissi’s email, “Do not mention Friday’s warning” · Place checker: the timeline | **Friday** |

Write a note to the head teacher: “The roof was dangerous since… Ms Diop… Mr El Idrissi…”

Ending: Karina is safe; Ms Diop helped her; Mr El Idrissi delayed the repair and told her to keep quiet. Ask the class: “Was Ms Diop right to lie?” Accept different opinions with reasons.

## Helping a stuck team

- **Name the two roles** the current question asks for, and ask each of them: “What does your picture show?”
- **News comes by itself.** With auto-advance on, each chapter’s two tips arrive when half the class is stuck. Each tip names the two roles to compare. You can also release them with **Next news**.
- **Writing:** accept short, simple sentences with the facts; there is no word count. If an automatic check is uncertain, accept the report in Teacher controls.
