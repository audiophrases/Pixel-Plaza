# Castanyada witness investigations

In Teacher controls, start **Castanyada: Witness Investigations** for a new game. This five-chapter template requires an exchange with a classmate before each main conclusion; the thief question requires replies from two distinct witnesses. Existing saved campaigns keep their pinned main objectives. Mission Studio can also require one or two witnesses on individual objectives.

The student's journal shows the question, eligible online witnesses and their own private clues. The investigator chooses a witness, writes a question about the mystery and attaches a private clue. The question goes through the same checks as chat (blocked words, the harm check and, with a key, OpenAI) and posts at once: the witness sees the question and evidence in their journal, writes a reply and shares one of their own unlocked private clues. Once the reply has posted, the investigator can submit a conclusion. Nothing waits for the teacher. Two-witness tasks require at least three students; one-witness tasks require at least two. There is no automatic solo bypass.

Interview and information-exchange board quests require the same exchange with the assigned partner before a report counts, including in existing campaigns. Both students contribute private evidence. After participation is verified, reports of at least five words receive an automatic task check and one short feedback message. Uncertain answers stay saved for teacher review. Teachers can read every question and reply, take down any that are off task, and assess the conclusion themselves.

Ordinary chat, self-interviews, guessed answers, fabricated clue IDs and another student's reply do not earn participation. The server supplies the attached evidence from the sender's actual journal. Witness evidence stays hidden from the investigator until the witness replies, and other classmates cannot see the transcript. Exchanges are scoped to a game, chapter, objective and investigator, so they cannot be reused for another question. A replacement partner requires a new exchange with that partner.

Teacher controls includes **Witness exchanges** under the campaign. It shows the task, participants, question, reply, evidence and status, with **Remove question** and **Remove reply**. Removing a turn revokes its participation credit for an unfinished conclusion; it does not erase conclusions already submitted. A removed question can be rewritten, and a removed reply can be revised by the original witness.

Exchanges and reports survive reconnects and server restarts. A conclusion remains available if a witness goes offline after the exchange. Resetting a campaign or deleting its game clears its exchanges. These records demonstrate an in-game exchange, not spoken conversation or comprehension. Classroom pacing and the relevance of student contributions still need teacher observation.
